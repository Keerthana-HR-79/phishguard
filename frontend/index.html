How it works

1. ML model
XGBoost scores every URL from 13 engineered URL features plus TF-IDF character patterns.
2. Live blocklist
URLs reported to OpenPhish or PhishTank are flagged as phishing. The OpenPhish feed refreshes every 6 hours.
3. Trusted-domain allowlist
Well-known legitimate domains are marked safe, so they never get a false alarm.
4. Risk score
A raw IP host, the "@" trick or a brand look-alike is flagged outright. Otherwise the model's score plus brand and new-domain checks decide: phishing, suspicious or safe.
